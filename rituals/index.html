Your 2027 Ritual Calendar

Every moon phase, season and retrograde of 2027, each with one ritual. Under fifteen minutes, using what is already in your house. Times are Eastern.

January 2027

Jan 7

🌑 New Moon in Capricorn

3:24 PM ET

Write the goal on a small piece of paper and fold it once. Put it in the soil of a houseplant, or under a stone outside where it will not get moved.

You are not asking for it. You are putting it somewhere it can take its time.

Come back on July 18, when the full moon lands in Capricorn and shows you what grew.

Jan 15

🌓 First Quarter Moon in Aries

3:34 PM ET

First quarter moons are the friction point. Something started and now it is harder.

Write the one thing you have been avoiding, then do the smallest version of it today.

Jan 20

✨ Aquarius Season Begins

2:30 AM ET

Write down one thing you want to try that you have not seen anyone else doing. Then send one message to one person you have been meaning to reach.

One question to sit with this month:

Where am I following a rule that nobody actually gave me?

Jan 22

🌕 Full Moon in Leo

7:17 AM ET

Light a candle and put it where you can see your own face.

Say out loud the one thing you have done this year that you are proud of. All the way through, no qualifier on the end.

Leo full moons are for being witnessed. If nobody is home, you count.

Jan 29

🌗 Last Quarter Moon in Scorpio

5:55 AM ET

Write what it was holding for you.
